Background

From Enterprise Biotechnology to Velocity

Craig Levinson spent more than twenty-five years in leadership roles across the biotechnology industry, including positions at Cytiva (formerly GE Healthcare Life Sciences), Sigma-Aldrich, Waters Corporation, and Packard Bioscience. That career was defined by complex strategic sales, high-performing team development, and multimillion-dollar portfolio management inside organizations built on evidence, measurement, and rigor.

The founding of Velocity Performance and Wellness is best understood as the application of that background rather than a break from it. Levinson did not arrive at wellness through fitness culture or consumer marketing; he arrived through an industry that required data before it accepted a claim, and that origin still shapes how Velocity operates today. The full story is told in the founding of Velocity Performance and Wellness.

Philosophy

Intelligent Performance, Not More Exercise

Craig's central philosophy challenges a common assumption in the wellness industry: that more exercise produces better health. Sustainable performance, in his view, comes from precision, not volume, balancing training load, recovery, and stress management against objective measurement.

This is why Levinson frames Velocity's mission around healthspan, the duration of healthy, active life, rather than short-term fitness metrics. Clients are guided toward decisions supported by data rather than assumption, an approach drawn directly from his years evaluating evidence in a scientific setting.

Technology

Diagnostics-Driven, Data-Informed Wellness

Velocity Performance and Wellness integrates advanced performance technologies, recovery therapies, and diagnostics into a single, measurable experience. Craig Levinson selects and applies these tools with the same rigor he once brought to evaluating life-science technologies for enterprise portfolios, rather than chasing wellness trends.

Personalization at Velocity is data-driven rather than assumption-driven. Diagnostics establish a baseline, technology tracks change over time, and Craig uses those objective benchmarks, not guesswork, to adjust each client's plan. More on how his biotech career shaped this approach is available in that article.

Model

Built for the Busy Professional

Velocity's appointment-based model is a deliberate design choice rather than a scheduling convenience. Craig Levinson built the practice around clients who have demanding schedules and need measurable outcomes within constrained time, not open-ended programs.

That structure reflects the operational discipline Levinson developed as a life-sciences executive: consultative engagement, clear benchmarks, and efficient use of a client's time. Velocity was built with that same discipline, not the enthusiasm-first approach common to many wellness startups.

Indiana Roots

A Local Voice in Health Technology

Craig Levinson and Velocity Performance and Wellness are rooted in Carmel, Indiana, serving local professionals, health-conscious individuals, and busy executives across the Indianapolis metro area who are looking for measurable wellness outcomes.

As the broader wellness industry shifts toward diagnostics, recovery science, and personalized health data, a direction Levinson has been oriented toward professionally for decades, he has positioned himself as a credible, accessible voice in Carmel's emerging health technology landscape.
