The Translation

A Biotech Career, Applied to Wellness

Craig Levinson built his career inside enterprise biotechnology, holding leadership roles at organizations including Cytiva, Sigma-Aldrich, Waters Corporation, and Packard Bioscience. Twenty-five years of complex strategic sales, portfolio management, and team development gave him a close view of the technologies shaping modern medicine and research.

Velocity Performance and Wellness is not a departure from that career - it is its natural next chapter. Craig Levinson founded the Carmel, Indiana practice to apply the same standards of evidence and measurement to performance, recovery, and personalized wellness that he once applied to enterprise life-science portfolios.

The result is an appointment-based model built for busy professionals who want outcomes they can track, not routines that demand unlimited time. Craig's approach to intelligent training and healthspan is detailed further on the about page.
